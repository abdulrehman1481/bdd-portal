The System

HOW IT
Actually Works

From a critical request to a matched donor — in under two minutes. Here's the engine behind BloodLink.

01
Register & Verify
Donors verify via phone. Hospitals go through our facility verification workflow before gaining access to the donor radar.
02
Request Created
A hospital or family member creates a blood request with patient details, blood type, and urgency level (Normal / High / Critical).
03
Geo-Match Engine
PostGIS queries all eligible donors — correct blood type, 90-day cooldown elapsed — within a configurable radius.
04
SOS Ping Sent
Matched donors receive an instant push notification. First to accept triggers a confirmation flow and gets hospital directions.
